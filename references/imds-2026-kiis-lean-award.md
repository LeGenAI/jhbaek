Article summary:
Sogang IMDS reported that Youngjik Lee and four coauthors — Jon-Lark Kim, Woo-Seok Jeong, Beom-Soo Kim, and Jae-Hyun Baek — received a Best Paper Award at the 2026 Spring Conference of the Korean Institute of Intelligent Systems. The article frames the presentation as the first official presentation signaling that IMDS Lean research has entered a full trajectory and as a signal for continued work in AI for Math. The work compares a closed-agent system for math problem solving: a Single architecture using ordinary solving strategies and a Dual architecture where agents debate with a falsifier. It evaluates token/compute efficiency and final solving performance.
